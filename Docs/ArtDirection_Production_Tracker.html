01 · Visual direction

Grounded materials. Readable form. Fantasy scale.

References guide construction and material response; they do not make assets current-game art or certify integration.

Approved visual direction

A lived-in fantasy world that grows from an ordinary, materially grounded beginning toward mythic scale. Keep regions coherent. Favor visible construction, weathered surfaces, believable anatomy, readable face and gaze, layered clothing, and equipment that sits naturally. Creature anatomy should read at its scale; magic should have clear charge, release, and impact forms.

Bramblekin request: a cuter Yordle-archetype silhouette with layered hair/fur obscuring head-to-body separation. Current upper-body proof with hair and neck ruff is still needed before more baking.
Adult character direction: clearly adult, idealized/readably feminine anatomy; cleavage is allowed. Anatomically accurate adult base geometry/textures beneath clothing are allowed. Visible gameplay remains non-explicit and R18-free; use clothing coverage and editor-visibility controls. Hidden unused anatomy details alone are not a release blocker or rebuild trigger. Plan restrained, damped, clothing-aware secondary motion and editor-compatible body/garment fit; define bone/morph cost and start/stop/settling checks before implementation. Planning only; no implementation or approval.
