How to use these examples

Use the publisher images as targets for shape, materials, atmosphere, and visual quality. Captions suggest coherent uses for the packs; they do not establish new locations, lore, or gameplay requirements. Keep a region’s architecture and palette consistent as you curate it.

The current GDD’s opening remains Archivist Vey’s warm, finished hut. The earlier integration plan’s mansion slice contributes an interior-quality reference. Solo Leveling and The Beginning After the End inform the GDD’s awakening and progression fantasy; the purchased art supplies the material and character language.

These are product showcases, not images of the current playable build. Some publisher scenes may use HDRP, additional demo content, or a newer asset version. Local pack roots were checked. Gabriel Aguiar’s local vendor root contains documentation; integrated effect prefabs were not verified. Spell-ring behavior and in-game visual cohesion were not tested in this compilation.
