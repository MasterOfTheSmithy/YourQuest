YOURQUEST — ART DIRECTION EXAMPLES
Compiled 3 October 2026

START HERE
Open index.html for the visual reference board. Images are also organized into
four numbered folders so they can be browsed directly without a web connection.
source_manifest.json records the publisher page, exact image URL, local pack
root, image dimensions, and checksum for every example.
LOCAL_PROJECT_FILES.txt indexes 15 canonical Unity sample scenes or pack docs
already in Assets. Their source files remain in their original locations.

INTENDED LOOK
A detailed, materially grounded fantasy world with weathered settlements,
rich interiors, culturally distinct regions, threatening creatures, and a
clear escalation from an ordinary beginning to mythic magical power.
This is a visual synthesis of the purchased packs and the current GDD, offered
for review; the captions describe plausible art uses rather than new canon.

ENVIRONMENTS
Befour Studios: Medieval Viking Village, Medieval Nordic Village, Victorian
Mansion, Asian Dynasty, and Persepolis Empire.
HIVEMIND: Medieval Kingdom and Gothic Cathedral.
Use each architectural family coherently within a location. Bring their scale,
material response, lighting, and ground contact together during integration.
The current GDD places the opening in Archivist Vey's warm, finished hut.
The older AssetIntegrationPlan nominates Victorian Mansion as a first hero
slice; that is a useful interior-quality reference and does not relocate the
GDD's starting scene.

CHARACTERS, EQUIPMENT, AND CREATURES
Infinity PBR / Magic Pig Games: Human Character, Weapons & Armor Pack 1,
Mimics & Chests, and Dragons. These are the high-detail pack references whose
roots are present in the project. Equipment should read clearly on the same
player in first-person and third-person views.

MAGIC
Gabriel Aguiar Productions: Unique AoE Magic Abilities Vol. 1, listed in the
approved palette. The local vendor root contains documentation; this folder
does not certify that the effect prefabs are integrated. Curate elemental
families and readable charge/release/impact shapes. The GDD specifies spell
circles 1–7 with one animated casting ring per circle; the publisher effects
are quality/color references, not proof that those rings are implemented.

VISUAL PRINCIPLES
- Grounded materials: timber, stone, cloth, leather, metal, and organic forms.
- Warm, welcoming light in safe interiors; clear sightlines for interaction.
- Regional identity through architecture, palette, and local set dressing.
- Strong silhouettes and readable encounters in ominous or monumental spaces.
- Focused magical highlights with visible casting, release, and impact phases.
- Progression-fantasy scale from Solo Leveling / The Beginning After the End,
  as named in the GDD; this does not prescribe a cel-shaded rendering style.

REFERENCE BOUNDARY
The images are publisher product showcases downloaded on the compilation date,
not screenshots of the current playable game. Marketplace images may show
HDRP lighting, other demo content, or a newer package release than the local
import. Use them as visual targets; verify the chosen local version in URP
when implementing. No models, textures, prefabs, scenes, or saves were changed.
UI examples and DOT-generated assets are excluded from this folder.

SOURCE NOTES
Product pages are linked beneath each pack on the board and in the manifest.
Local roots establish that the corresponding pack material is present; purchase
history was supplied by the user and was not audited. Original publisher image
bytes are retained. The reference folder is for the project's internal review.
