## 1. World composition

Source: [NPS Dinosaur plant communities](https://www.nps.gov/dino/learn/nature/plant-communities.htm), National Park Service; text updated December 16, 2024. Reference location: Montane Forest and Montane/Semi-Desert Woodlands sections; photo captions identify Harpers Corner and Plug Hat Butte, credited to Paul Souders. Real landscape; game/platform not applicable. Photo retrieval failed, so visual inspection of those photos is still pending.

Observed text principle: elevation, slope, exposure, soil and moisture distinguish habitats. YourQuest application is an inference: use readable connected landforms and habitat mixtures tied to existing macro inputs, with distinct foreground, middle distance and skyline. Do not copy the park's species/altitude thresholds into the fantasy terrain.

Test: matched ordinary views along a ridge/valley/pass and accepted-origin transition, in both cameras and four varied seeds. Record landmark silhouette, route readability and terrain/paint/collision seams independently. [Unity Terrain.SetNeighbors](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/Terrain.SetNeighbors.html), Unity Technologies, 6000.3 documentation, concerns neighbor LOD linkage; height/paint/collision agreement still needs separate evidence.

## 2. Ecology

Source: the same NPS page, references Montane Shrub-Steppe, Semi-Desert Shrub-Steppe and Riparian Woodlands/Wetlands. The named text differentiates open shrub/grass mosaics from moist river corridors. Photo reference: Jones Hole Creek, Paul Souders; photo access unverified.

YourQuest interpretation: canopy, shrub/understory and ground layers must form appropriate mixtures and quiet clearings. Water proximity and existing land use should influence edge habitats without inventing water topology. Preserve approved vendor families rather than mixing incompatible art at random.

Test: actual TemperateLowland, AncientWoodland, Highland, Moorland and Wetland samples; supported pairwise transitions; measured age/scale/rotation diversity, grounded bases and exclusions around doors/routes/anchors. Sparse distant trees alone do not prove a complete layered habitat.

## Original YourQuest direction and scoring rule

Use structured region identity to select coherent approved art families. Make large connected relief and landmarks readable, keep low-level habitat variation within a consistent material family, and give water/routes/built land a physical reason to meet. Maintain deliberate quiet wilderness between meaningful places. Interiors should reveal functions through layout and reachable anchors. Retain vendor material intent and original YourQuest world identities; do not recreate reference signature layouts.

Before composition rollout, finish actual family/source approval review and image inspection for each supported palette. The two inspected project images and seven publisher examples below are baseline defects/style references, not a full-world art direction certification.
