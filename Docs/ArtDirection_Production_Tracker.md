## Quality gates and budgets

- **Visual acceptance:** judge the actual current model/material in a reviewable view; reference-board resemblance or a generated concept alone is not approval. Review anatomy, face/gaze, garment fit and attachment, material response, silhouette, contact, and presentation lighting. For the player, check first- and third-person views of the same player.
- **Adult-character and motion planning:** characters must be clearly adults; anatomically accurate adult base geometry/textures beneath clothing are permitted. Visible gameplay presentation remains non-explicit and R18-free; clothing coverage and editor-visibility controls remain. Hidden, unused anatomy details alone are not a release blocker or rebuild trigger. Plan restrained, damped, clothing-aware secondary motion and define bone/morph ownership, runtime cost, start/stop blending, settling, and garment/contact checks before implementation.
- **Technical acceptance:** keep budget, LOD, rig, animation transitions, body/contact, schema, tangent, native-import, hash, and visual-review evidence distinct. Preserve disclosed residuals and warnings; do not omit required content to pass a gate. A newly found defect in a delivered package holds correction/release until its changed-file repair is verified.

Schema/technical passes alone do not authorize delivery; never infer aesthetic approval. WIP images remain previews, not model downloads.
